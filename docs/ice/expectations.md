## EXPECTATIONS — what counts as done (in the shopper's words; owned by the PO)

Done — counts when the shopper can say:

- "I got a recipe I could actually make tonight from things I can buy right here."
- "It told me exactly the few extra things to grab, with amounts."
- "It answered in French, quickly, and I didn't have to sign up for anything."
- "It worked around the fact that I'm [vegetarian / nut-allergic / avoiding pork]."
- "It suggested something that fit [the match tonight / the weekend] without nagging."
- "I decided everything — it just made deciding easier."

Failed — even if the answer looks nice, when the shopper would say:

- "It gave me a recipe needing stuff this shop doesn't carry."
- "It ignored that I told it I can't eat something."
- "It told me a price, or said something was 'on offer,' and that wasn't true."
- "It acted like it had bought things for me."
- "It kept pushing a product unrelated to my meal."
- "It was so slow, or asked so many questions, that shopping normally was easier."
- "It answered in English, or talked about things that aren't food or shopping."

Limits — it must stay inside:

- It advises; the shopper always decides. It never buys or commits anything.
- It mentions a price or deal only if real and current; otherwise it says to check in store.
- It never includes anything the shopper said they can't eat.
- It stays about food, cooking, and this shop — nothing else.
- French, short enough to read while standing in an aisle.
